# Best contact lens reminder apps for iPhone (2026)

> Nine contact lens reminder and tracker apps for iPhone compared by US rating count and by what
> happens on day 15, when you have forgotten the app exists. Written by Necati Dogrul, who
> develops LensLoop — last on this list. That conflict of interest is disclosed throughout, and
> LensLoop is placed last because it has the fewest ratings.

Updated 12 September 2026. Rating counts read from the US App Store via the public iTunes lookup API.

## The one thing most lists get wrong

This is a tiny category. The most-rated contact lens tracker in the US has 945 ratings after ten
years on the store; everything below it is in the double digits or lower. "Most popular" tells
you little, and the retail apps in the same search (1-800 Contacts, 140,000+ ratings) are shops,
not trackers.

What separates these apps is what happens on day 15: does the reminder repeat until you act;
can you see the countdown without opening the app (widget, Lock Screen, Watch); does it treat
your two eyes as two lenses. A fourth thing almost nobody tracks: how the lenses feel. People
quit contacts because of a slow slide into dryness and irritation that no calendar shows.

## How to choose

- Most-used, no surprises: Contact Lenses Tracker by DOUBLE U (free, nags until you replace, ten years of updates; Premium twin $29.99 up front).
- Home Screen first: Contact Lens Reminder – Widget, the runaway leader in Japan (nearly 4,000 ratings); days remaining, replace-from-widget, calendar widget.
- Pay once, no subscription: Lensy ($1.99) or DOUBLE U Premium ($29.99).
- Comfort matters as much as the date: LensLoop, a one-tap daily comfort check with a trend. This is what LensLoop was built for.

## What to check before you install

- Per-eye tracking. - Repeat reminders ("reminds until replaced"). - A glanceable surface: widget, Lock Screen or Watch. - Accessories: solution, case, drops. - Last updated: two apps in the results have not shipped since 2017.
