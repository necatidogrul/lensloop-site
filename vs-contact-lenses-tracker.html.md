By Necati Dogrul, developer of LensLoop · 12 September 2026 · https://necatidogrul.github.io/lensloop-site/vs-contact-lenses-tracker.html

> Disclosure: written by the developer of one of the two apps. Sections where Contact Lenses
> Tracker is stronger are stated explicitly.

**Short answer:** Contact Lenses Tracker (DOUBLE U) is the safe pick: ten years on the store,
the most ratings in the category, reminders that keep nagging until you replace the pair.
LensLoop is the better pick if you want the countdown on your wrist or Lock Screen, track
accessories and your prescription in the same place, or want to see how your eyes feel over time.

## Where Contact Lenses Tracker is genuinely better

- Track record: nearly a thousand ratings and updates since 2016. LensLoop shipped in July 2026.
- Persistence: reminders repeat until you mark the pair replaced. LensLoop reminds the day before and on the day.
- Pay-once option at the top: the $29.99 Premium app is a single purchase.

## Where LensLoop is better

- Glanceable everywhere: Home and Lock Screen widgets, full Apple Watch app with complications.
- Two eyes, honestly: left and right separately, or as one pair.
- Comfort, not only dates: one-tap daily comfort check with a trend.
- The whole routine: solution, case, drops, prescription photo and exam date in one app.
- Free tier without ads: first pair free forever.
